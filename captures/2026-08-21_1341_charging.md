### Energy conservation

Off grid, so `PV in = load out + battery charge` with no other source:

| Assumption | In | Out | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| nothing scaled | 2055 W | 2485 W | **-430 W, impossible** |
| PV and load x2, BMS whole-bank | 4110 W | 3383 W | +727 W (17.7% losses) |
| PV and load x2, BMS x2 | 4110 W | 4969 W | **-859 W, impossible** |
| nothing scaled, inverter ammeter | 2055 W | 1059 W | +996 W (48.5%) |
| PV and load x2, inverter ammeter x2 | 4110 W | 2118 W | +1992 W (48.5%) |

Two require energy from nowhere at this instant. But the same test on the repo's own
`CAPTURE_TELEMETRY` fixture gives **110.0% efficiency** under the surviving model, and

are not co-sampled, which invalidates instantaneous balance reasoning generally.

The honest test needs no new capture: integrate `c_generation_power_w` over 24 hours with
a Riemann-sum helper and compare against the device's own `pv_today_kwh`. Averaging over a
day removes the sampling noise that makes the instant test swing between 82% and 110%.
